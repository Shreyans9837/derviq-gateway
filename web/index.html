AI models produce decisions. Applications produce side effects. Derviq is designed around the boundary between the two.

11 / Questions

Clear answers, no magic.

Who is the initial buyer?

Teams already deploying action-capable agents: agent platforms, enterprise AI teams, SaaS automation products and sensitive operational workflows. The next proof is willingness to pay in design-partner deployments.

Does the public demo move real money?

No. The public console evaluates control decisions and evidence paths; it does not perform real financial settlement.

Is the evidence chain immutable?

The current mechanism is a tamper-evident SHA-256 hash chain. True WORM storage or hardware/KMS-backed custody would require additional infrastructure.

Can Derviq roll back any agent action?

No. Recovery is supported where a defined compensation path exists. Universal rollback across arbitrary external systems is not claimed.

What proves the next stage?

Real design partners, measurable blocked-risk events, approval behavior, reliability metrics, integration depth and repeat usage.
